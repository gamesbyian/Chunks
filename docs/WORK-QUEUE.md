## Milestone gates and acceptance

- **G0: actual integrity and viability (P0).** No reachable unintended deletion, path escape, internal metadata overwrite/resurrection, cross-account command or credential disclosure in supported enabled paths. Distinguish global core hazards from a demonstrably disabled unsafe optional executor; follow the [rationality review](REQUIREMENTS-RATIONALITY-AUDIT-2026-10-31.md). Source code, host tests, emulator tests and physical proof are separately labeled.
- **G1: ordinary torrent operations (P0).** Independently qualify **local-only** and **remote-only** usable paths: Add/Start/Stop/priorities/session Remove (where applicable), piece-verified bytes, resume/recheck and honest uncertain/outage states. Both get their own acceptance receipts; neither automatically proves combined-mode or indefinite background seeding. Physical device and supported live host are explicit additional tiers.
- **G2: one coherent app (P0).** Real phone and multiple remote profiles share transfer identity, query, scope, UI semantics and controls; offline/unknown states and default Place remain honest. Accept Android Share, magnet and .torrent entries, untouched optional settings, easy file access, 320×700 and 412×892 at 200%, TalkBack, keyboard and process restoration. [Unified environments](UNIFIED-ENVIRONMENTS.md), [UX](UX.md), [#779](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/779).
- **G3: recoverable file features (P1).** Enable independent ZIP/library publication, verified backing rename/relocation, quotas, snapshot recovery and external destination delivery **one provider/operation at a time** after actual permission and positive/hostile readback. Keep torrent-job Copy/Move separate from payload copy/rename and preserve originals by default. [Storage](STORAGE-AND-EXTRACTION.md), [user-directed operations](USER-DIRECTED-FILE-OPERATIONS.md), [external delivery](EXTERNAL-DESTINATION-DELIVERY.md).
- **G4: advanced behavior (P2).** Preserve regression coverage for existing [Finish in Order](INTAKE-QUEUE-AND-POLICIES.md), [smart seeding](SMART-SEEDING-RULES.md), [Create & Share](CREATE-AND-SHARE.md) and organization. Broader discovery, policy engines, event automation, provider catalogs, file integrations and future backend adapters follow the tested G1/G2 identities.
- **G5: future/editorial (P3).** [Artwork](TRANSFER-ARTWORK.md), [widgets](PER-TRANSFER-HOME-WIDGETS.md), [Android TV and multi-backend future](ANDROID-TV-AND-REMOTE-BACKENDS.md), and typography beyond approved ExtraBold are owner-approved requirements, **deferred not cancelled**. Font experimentation remains isolated on `font/lab`. Signed distribution, security and accessibility do not wait until G5; they are continuous [#10](https://github.com/gamesbyian/Chunks/issues/10) gates.

## Stop and escalation rules

1. A red required check is **not a license to merge**, but classify *why*: product assertion, compilation, harness script, emulator boot/runner, or missing acceptance. Repair and repeat the correct exact-head tier. A research link test does not count as an installed Android feature; a boot failure is not evidence of an app bug. [CI-POLICY](CI-POLICY.md).
2. If two iterations or a major design expansion produce no positive shipping-progress receipt, **re-evaluate the original premise and conventional library/platform mechanism**. This review trigger does not waive confirmed harm. Do not extend closed #568 research solely to satisfy the abandoned non-clobber policy.
3. A feature is **implemented** only if its production entry path works with representative correct outcomes, recovery and scope-specific negatives. A dormant provider, pure DTO, headless simulator or disconnected experiment proves only its own tier. Complete conventional user journeys early; avoid endless negative-only hardening.
4. Retire a superseded PR only after comparing unique code/tests against main and recording ported/rejected disposition. Prefer one clean-main integration PR; keep scope and review manageable. Do not re-merge old stacked branches or multiply doc-only status PRs.
5. When hardware, protected hosts or human accessibility validation are unavailable, keep those limits open and work on another unblocked **G0–G2** deliverable. Do not declare comprehensive beta/physical acceptance from emulator/fixture success, and do not fill the wait with unrelated optional features.
6. After every qualified merge, reconcile the exact source SHA/CI, issue status, [coverage](SPEC-COVERAGE.md) and [quality evidence](QUALITY-LEDGER.md); name one next executable unclaimed task. Apply [CI cost boundaries](CI-POLICY.md); no docs-only native/font builds or unsecured branch assumptions.
