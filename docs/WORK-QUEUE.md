## Stop and escalation rules

1. A red required check is **not a license to merge**, but classify *why*: product assertion, compilation, harness script, emulator boot/runner, or missing acceptance. Repair and repeat the correct exact-head tier. A research link test does not count as an installed Android feature; a boot failure is not evidence of an app bug. [CI-POLICY](CI-POLICY.md).
2. If two iterations or a major design expansion produce no positive shipping-progress receipt, **re-evaluate the original premise and conventional library/platform mechanism**. This review trigger does not waive confirmed harm. Do not extend closed #568 research solely to satisfy the abandoned non-clobber policy.
3. A feature is **implemented** only if its production entry path works with representative correct outcomes, recovery and scope-specific negatives. A dormant provider, pure DTO, headless simulator or disconnected experiment proves only its own tier. Complete conventional user journeys early; avoid endless negative-only hardening.
4. Retire a superseded PR only after comparing unique code/tests against main and recording ported/rejected disposition. Prefer one clean-main integration PR; keep scope and review manageable. Do not re-merge old stacked branches or multiply doc-only status PRs.
5. When hardware, protected hosts or human accessibility validation are unavailable, keep those limits open and work on another unblocked **G0–G2** deliverable. Do not declare comprehensive beta/physical acceptance from emulator/fixture success, and do not fill the wait with unrelated optional features.
6. After every qualified merge, reconcile the exact source SHA/CI, issue status, [coverage](SPEC-COVERAGE.md) and [quality evidence](QUALITY-LEDGER.md); name one next executable unclaimed task. Apply [CI cost boundaries](CI-POLICY.md); no docs-only native/font builds or unsecured branch assumptions.
