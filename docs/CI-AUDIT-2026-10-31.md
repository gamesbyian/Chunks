## Required-check and security qualifications

Repository ruleset enumeration returned no rulesets, but the integration received HTTP **403** when attempting the branch-protection endpoint. Therefore required-status contexts **cannot be certified as unconfigured**. A combined job may need settings migration before removing an old check context; do not infer this from the absence of visible rulesets. All protected Seedhost/release workflows must remain manual or trusted-main-scoped and secret-free on public PRs. A passing offline harness cannot certify a protected account or physical handset.

## Improvement loop and next dispatch

1. **Completed:** #875 exact-head docs, radio, JVM and API35 checked and merged; #877 immutable signing-action references exact-head offline checked and merged.
2. Add tested path precision to Android foundation: avoid building a Debug APK for an androidTest-only change already checked by API35 instrumentation, and move tool-only mock/preflight changes to the cheap validation workflow.
3. Measure identical-source cold/warm API35 jobs and SDK/toolchain installation separately from Gradle compilation and emulator boot. Only then decide whether merging stages or sharing immutable APKs saves more time than artifact transfer and reduces critical-path duration.
4. Extend bounded failure indices to API29 and R8 with real report locations and verify their first-failure usefulness. Protect JUnit test execution truth; never let a summary-writing error conceal the product failure.
5. Review action pinning, automatic protected-host one-off dispatch, historical font push duplication and branch protection before subsequent workflow removals.

Every claim of saved runner minutes or latency requires comparable actual before/after samples; a mathematical reduction in eligible dispatch count is not yet such evidence.
