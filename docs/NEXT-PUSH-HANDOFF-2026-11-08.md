2. **Native checking/partial resume.** **Forced stopped-recheck ordering is fixed and proven deterministically (#1889):** `StoppedCheck.begin` runs `forceRecheck`, `resume`, THEN arms `stop_when_ready` (libtorrent's immediate-stop shortcut otherwise lets the following `resume()` undo the stop), and `StoppedCheckTest` models the documented semantics with the check completion at every point of the sequence. Use it for every stopped check; never add another `forceRecheck/setFlags/resume` triple. **Diagnosed and fixed (2026-11-19):** the `startAfterPayloadTruncated…` `repaired-download-flush` timeout was libtorrent's peer reconnect backoff, not a recheck or flush defect.
- **Evidence:** a local reproduction (1 of 6 runs) showed zero peers for the whole 60 s wait after the seeder's pause/resume closed the loopback connection.
- **Mechanism:** upstream `peer_list::find_connect_candidates` skips a peer for `(failcount + 1) × min_reconnect_time` (default 60 s). It does not apply when `last_connected == 0`, i.e. when the close happened in the session's first second. That is why fast runs passed and loaded runs failed. `connect_peer` only re-adds the peer to that list.
- **Fix:** a test-only `LocalEngineConfig.minReconnectSeconds = 1`; the app keeps libtorrent's default. 8 of 8 loaded local runs then passed, each about 6.4 s.
- **Still to do:** record CI pass rates after merge. Apply the same reasoning to any other same-host stop/resume reconnect test.
