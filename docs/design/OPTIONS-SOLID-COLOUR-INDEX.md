Use the actual Chunks branding, supported system insets, and existing theme/font system. ## Visual direction

- **Dark chassis, solid-colour category cards.** Replace black cards with coloured outlines by substantive filled surfaces, one category colour per card, with dark enough effective fill for readable white/near-white text. Keep a subtle optional tone-on-tone border or elevation shadow for boundaries; do **not** revert to outline-only treatment. Avoid neon-bright full-card fills behind white text, illegible colour combinations, or large gradients that read as black cards. A restrained monochromatic gradient *within the category colour* is permitted only if its entire visible area still reads as a solid colour card. Distinct cards may use the Chunks rainbow family: purple, yellow/gold, cyan, lime, pink, orange/amber, turquoise, violet; hues must be tuned and tested rather than treating example hexes as guaranteed accessible.
- **Readable filled cards:** Use a category hue with an appropriately deep shade (for example purple #653184, yellow-ochre #765A00, cyan-teal #006C80, lime-green #376E0C, pink-plum #8C2664, amber-brown #81500E, teal #006F70, violet #63358E); these are *starting visual candidates* only, not certified accessibility tokens. Icon-disc may use a brighter shade within the same category family, paired with a strongly contrasting glyph. Validate text/glyph contrast against all actual rendered surfaces in light and dark themes. Dark icons on bright discs are fine. Card body text must be readable at normal and enlarged sizes, and text contrast cannot rely on decorative outlines.
- A full-width rounded card has: circular category icon at leading edge; **bold title** (up to two lines where needed); short two-line maximum summary of actual available settings; trailing chevron *only when the card opens a destination*. Use no text/image duplicated within the card, no oversized empty margins. All visible card area acts as a single tap target.
- Header: preserve the actual Chunks wordmark/title and a clearly associated **Search options** field immediately beneath it, on the same dark canvas. Respect notification/system bar, IME, and cutout insets. No blacked-out placeholders are part of the design. The screenshot's bright ribbon wallpaper is external presentation framing, **not** an app background.
- Footer: persistent **Transfers / + Add / Connections / Options**, one row. Options is the sole selected tab when at root or nested Options screens; Add stays emphatic but unselected elsewhere. Footer must never overlap the final card or keyboard focus.
- Prefer compact rhythm that exposes many categories on the owner's Pixel 6 Pro while retaining proper text size, minimum 48dp touch targets, and content scrolling. Never force text truncation simply to imitate the number of visible cards in a static mockup. Use a single vertical scrolling area for the cards/search results, not several independent vertical settings stacks.

**Information architecture migration:** The older eight destinations (Clients & environments; Appearance & display; Artwork & media intelligence; Automation & rules; Notifications; Widgets & integrations; Files & storage; Help & about) are **superseded at the top-level index only**. Move their *existing settings* to the eight cards above with one canonical persisted owner, preserve capabilities and exact setting scopes, and provide compatible legacy deep-links/search aliases. Do not silently discard Widgets, rules, local-access security, integrations, or file-source handling. Dedicated Connections footer stays authoritative for adding/editing connections; Options Connections is navigation to its related settings.

## Top-level quick toggles

The screenshot suggests trailing switches for Seeding & limits and Notifications. **Do not replicate a switch by appearance alone.** Only show a top-level switch where a real independently defined *single* preference exists, its effect is explainable alongside the card, and the control is separately accessible without triggering navigation. Otherwise use a chevron, including for Seeding & limits until a coherent master rule is explicitly defined. A switch acts and persists immediately, indicates pending/failure when required, and never changes several unrelated policies through an invented boolean. A card's navigational region and switch must be non-overlapping and distinctly announced by TalkBack.

## Layout and interactive states

- Responsive card body with leading icon ~48–56dp diameter (adjust to text scale), title and subtitle stack, end affordance. Maintain adequate card padding, radius ~16–20dp, compact vertical spacing, and comfortable margins. Exact dp are starting design tokens to tune on-device, not pixel reproduction.
- Cards and search use stable semantic IDs and accessible labels stating destination, current effective settings where useful, and whether a quick switch is on/off. Focus outline must be detectable on saturated surfaces; disabled items must be truthful and explain why. Avoid nested tap ambiguities.
- On search, filter by both visible card titles and *nested setting synonyms*. Results must navigate to the canonical destination and focus/scroll the target. Include connection/client/environment/Smart Connect, save/download/storage/archive, seed/ratio/speed/tracker/routing, artwork/poster/metadata/provider, notification, theme/font/motion/widget/accessibility, privacy/network/Wi-Fi/permissions/biometrics, diagnostics/version/help/integrations. Preserve query and scroll when returning.
- Theme adaptation: preserve category identity when light theme/system theme active, but choose appropriate category shade, text/icon colour, contrast and system bars; do not force dark theme globally.
- No active setting group may disappear merely because its visual category changed. Current data/settings are the authority, not mockup status. Do not manufacture functions or claim unfinished features ship.

## Acceptance

1. Primary Pixel 6 Pro installed screenshot visually exhibits **eight clearly filled coloured cards**, correctly ordered, not dark cards with colourful outlines; real wordmark, Search options, one-row footer and selected Options state. Inspect entire scrolled page and dark/light/system themes.
2. All eight categories open their correct canonical destination and return with previous search/scroll/focus; each nested setting from the former IA remains reachable through navigation/search (explicit inventory migration mapping).
3. Effective contrast: WCAG 4.5:1 for normal-sized text, 3:1 for large text and essential graphical controls (where applicable); verify across card fills, icons, pressed/focused/disabled states, system themes. Visual evaluation on actual primary handset, not numeric-only confidence.
4. TalkBack traverses cards, Search and any real quick switches independently; minimum usable targets 48dp; scroll insets prevent footer overlap; IME/keyboard, enlarged type/reduced motion and portrait recreation do not lose state.
5. All preferences follow immediate apply and saved effective values; no visual mock switch becomes an inert or misleading control; no duplicate persistent settings store.
6. Compare installed visuals with owner's reference composition, recording deliberate differences (solid-filled cards, corrected card summaries, no fabricated top-level seeding switch, preserved categories and accessibility) before claiming acceptance.
