# Chunks Reference: independent T/L cap and foot masters

**Status:** nonshipping, not owner-approved typeface art. These are editable source-designed alternatives and do not replace the immutable 62-glyph reference alphabet, original ExtraBold, or production Android fonts.

## Design rationale

The preceding letter-design passes implemented four true weight-specific Bézier masters for each of uppercase `I`, lowercase `l`, lowercase `i`, and uppercase `H`. Two more useful connected silhouettes are uppercase **T** and **L**: wide cap plus descending shaft, and vertical shaft plus right-facing foot. Their joins, cap thickness, outside radii and shaft widths are deliberately specified **independently for Regular, Medium, Semibold and Bold**, rather than generated by automatic erosion of the heavyweight glyph.

Eight SVGs in `assets/fonts/source/reference-alphabet/weight-overrides/{400,500,600,700}/U+{0054,004C}.svg` each retain one continuous outline with native quadratic Bézier handles. In the T, an inset shaft runs downward from a horizontal, softened cap; in L, a softened lower foot grows out of the vertical stem with an editable curved joint. Individual cap/foot thicknesses, shaft width and corner radii increase across four grades. Source masters remain editable as regular standalone SVG paths.

The existing experimental CFF weight study builder imports **those exact curved SVG outlines** directly, rather than converting the masters into polygon samples. Its safety checks reject topology changes, bounds outside a 3px source envelope, excessive silhouette area and incorrect optical ink gaps. The expanded native-weight unit tests assert one connected contour, source-to-CFF hashes, no counters, at least eight native curve segments, and strictly progressive ink mass across all weights. The experiment retains all 856 mapped Unicode codepoints and unrelated-script shaping. Current tests also render 14/24/48/82px editorial comparisons and remeasure optical kerning.

These eight outlines bring the independently specified basic-alphabet design set to **6 of 62 glyphs per lighter weight**, subject to CI and visual acceptance. The other **56 of 62** remain outline derivations, not independently authored weight masters. No automatic rebuild of later Consolidated/Stacked/Mixed experiments is assumed; their source manifests remain separate provenance.
